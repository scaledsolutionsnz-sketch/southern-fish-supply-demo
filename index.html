Our story

A local chippy that takes fish seriously

Southern Fish Supply is an Invercargill takeaway built on one simple idea — get good fish, cook it fresh, and send people home happy. We're lucky to sit so close to some of the best seafood in the country, and we make the most of it.

From a quick lunchtime feed of fish & chips to a big Friday-night family pack, you get the same thing every time: hot, crisp and made to order by people who'd rather do it properly than do it fast.

  • Locally owned and run in Invercargill
  • Fresh fish and hand-cut chips, cooked when you order
  • Honest portions and prices that stack up
