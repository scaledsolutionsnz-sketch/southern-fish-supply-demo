Privacy Policy

How Southern Fish Supply handles the information you share with us.

Last updated: 2026. This is placeholder content for demonstration purposes and should be reviewed before going live.

1. Who we are

Southern Fish Supply is a fish & chip takeaway based in Invercargill, New Zealand. We respect your privacy and handle personal information in line with the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020.

2. What we collect

We only collect what we need to serve you. That may include:

  • Your name and contact details when you place an order or send an enquiry;
  • The content of any message or feedback you send us;
  • Basic, non-identifying website analytics if enabled.

3. How we use it

We use your information to take and prepare your order, respond to enquiries, handle feedback, and improve our service. We do not sell your personal information to anyone.

4. Forms & messages

Enquiry and feedback forms on this site are delivered to us by email through a third-party form service. Information you submit is used only to respond to you.

5. Sharing

We only share personal information where necessary to provide our service (for example, our email or form provider) or where required by law.

6. Storage & security

We take reasonable steps to keep your information secure and retain it only as long as needed for the purpose it was collected.

7. Your rights

You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, or request a correction. Contact us and we'll help.

8. Contact
